Overview & objectives

The Summit is a two-day national platform for advancing investment in healthcare infrastructure and building a competitive pharmaceutical manufacturing base. Sessions combine keynotes, panels, and structured B2B meetings.

  • ✓Connect local manufacturers with international partners
  • ✓Advance regulatory and quality frameworks
